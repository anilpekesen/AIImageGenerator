# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Shopify merchants, small brands, dropshippers, and store operators who need publishable product visuals without hiring a studio or writing prompts. They work inside Shopify Admin and need fast, predictable product media workflows.

## Product Purpose
Snap6 turns one product image into a category-specific 6-image commercial photo set. Success means merchants can select a product, choose or confirm the right photo set, generate consistent high-quality visuals, refine individual images, and save them back to the product gallery.

## Brand Personality
Practical, expert, direct. The product should feel like a focused Shopify admin tool, not a generic AI playground.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic AI image generator workflows that make merchants invent prompts from scratch. Avoid marketing-heavy screens inside the app, decorative UI, vague credit language, and inconsistent output promises.

## Design Principles
- Keep the workflow product-first: product selection, set choice, image generation, save.
- Make the expert photo recipe visible enough to build trust without exposing prompt complexity.
- Preserve predictable costs: credits must map clearly to actions.
- Prefer category-specific outputs over generic scenes.
- Keep Shopify-native patterns and Polaris conventions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Default to Shopify Polaris accessibility behavior. Use clear labels, keyboard-accessible controls, readable contrast, and reduced visual noise for repeated merchant workflows.
